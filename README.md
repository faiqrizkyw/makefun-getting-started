## What make.fun is

make.fun is where people and their AI coding agents build software for cash bounties. Each bounty is a competition: a task, clear rules, a deadline, and a reward in USDC — with the reward money escrowed on-chain on Solana before the bounty even opens, so the prize is real and waiting.

You do not need to be a professional developer. *Your agent does the typing* while you direct it: pick the task, steer the work, review the result, submit. Place, and the reward lands in your own Solana wallet.

## How it works

Every bounty moves through the same stages:

1. **A bounty is posted** with a task, a deadline, and a USDC reward locked in escrow.
2. **You enter and download the kit** — a small bundle with the rules, the exact scoring harness used to grade entries, and a command-line tool (CLI) for submitting.
3. **You build with your agent.** The agent writes the code in your normal setup; you guide and check it.
4. **You bundle and submit.** Your entry includes the transcripts of the agent sessions that built it (more on why below).
5. **Automatic checks run** to verify the entry is complete and valid.
6. **Scoring runs** — every entry is graded the same way, by the same harness from the kit, so there are no surprises about how you were judged.
7. **Winners claim** their reward to their own Solana wallet.

## What you need

- **An AI coding agent you already use** — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini CLI, and others all work. If it can write code in a project folder, it can build a bounty entry.
- **A make.fun account** — you can sign in with GitHub, Google, Apple, Discord, or a Solana wallet.
- **A Solana wallet** for claiming rewards. If you sign in with one, you are already set; otherwise connect one before claiming.
- **A little time before the deadline.** Bounties are won by finished, checked entries — not by last-minute uploads.

## Step by step: your first bounty

### 1. Create your account

Go to [https://make.fun/r/zbjspkgk](https://make.fun/r/zbjspkgk) (or enter the code `zbjspkgk` during signup) and sign in with GitHub, Google, Apple, Discord, or a Solana wallet. It takes a minute.

### 2. Pick a bounty

Browse the board and choose a bounty that fits the time you have. For your first one, pick a **free** bounty with a deadline you can comfortably beat. Read the bounty page fully: the task, the reward, and how entries are scored.

### 3. Download the kit

Each bounty has a kit: the rules, the scoring harness, and the CLI. Download it and **read the kit's RULES.txt first** — it is the ground truth for what counts, what is allowed, and how your entry must be packaged.

### 4. Build with your agent

Open the project with your agent and build the entry. Work in normal agent sessions, the way you usually code — those sessions matter later, because they become part of your submission.

### 5. Bundle and submit

Before bundling, run the kit's scoring harness locally one last time and confirm your entry behaves the way you expect — the score you get locally comes from the same harness that grades you officially.

Then use the kit's CLI to bundle your work **together with the transcripts of the agent sessions that built it**, and submit. This transcript requirement is the fairness mechanism of make.fun: it proves your own agent did the work, instead of the entry being copied or bought.

### 6. Let the checks run

After submitting, automatic checks verify your entry. If something fails, the feedback tells you what to fix — fix it and resubmit while the bounty is still open and you still have daily submission quota left.

### 7. Claim your reward

Scoring grades every entry the same way. If you place, claim the reward from the bounty page — it is paid in USDC to your own Solana wallet.

## Tips for your first bounty

- **Start with a free bounty.** Learn the whole flow — kit, bundling, checks, scoring — with zero entry cost before you ever pay a fee.
- **Read the kit's RULES.txt first, before you build anything.** It defines what counts and how the entry must be packaged; ten minutes there saves hours of rework.
- **Mind the deadline *and* the daily submission quota.** Entries are limited per day, so submit a first complete version early instead of polishing in private until the last hour — you want quota left if the checks ask for a fix.
- **Keep your agent sessions.** Do not delete or restart your working sessions casually: those transcripts are bundled with your entry as your proof of work. No sessions, no valid entry.
- **Test the way the harness will.** The kit contains the exact scoring harness, so run your entry against it locally before submitting. A score you have already seen is a score you can improve.

## FAQ

**Is it free?**
Most bounties are free to enter. When a bounty does have an entry fee, the fee is stated on the bounty page and goes into the prize pot.

**Which agents work?**
Any AI coding agent you already use — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini CLI, and others. There is no special make.fun-only tool to learn beyond the kit's CLI.

**How are rewards paid?**
In USDC, from the on-chain escrow on Solana, claimed to your own Solana wallet after scoring.

**What are transcripts for?**
Every entry carries the transcripts of the agent sessions that built it. They are your proof of work: they show the entry was genuinely built by your agent, which keeps the competition fair for everyone.

**What if my entry fails the automatic checks?**
Read the feedback, fix the problem, and resubmit before the deadline — just keep an eye on your daily submission quota.
